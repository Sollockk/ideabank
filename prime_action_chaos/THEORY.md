## 1. Result and provenance

There is an explicit way to connect the manuscript's logarithmic arithmetic
action to chaotic evolution. It requires a **scale-shifting operator** in
addition to the independent phase rotations of Chapters 147--151.

The construction has two complementary parts:

1. On positive Fourier modes, integer dilation is an isometry satisfying
   `[K_0,U_m]=(log m)U_m`, with `K_0 e_n=(log n)e_n`. The same `U_m` is the
   Koopman pullback of the expanding map `x -> m x mod 1`.
2. A normalized diagonal state with eigenvalues `w_n` admits a deterministic
   full-branch interval map and an invertible baker extension whose
   branchwise logarithmic stretching is exactly `-log w_n`. With finite
   entropy, their positive Lyapunov exponent and metric entropy are
   `S(w)=-sum_n w_n log w_n` per iteration, almost everywhere.

For the arithmetic Gibbs state

$$
w_n(s)=\frac{n^{-s}}{\zeta(s)},\qquad s>1,
$$

this gives

$$
\boxed{\lambda_+(s)=h_{\rm KS}(s)=S(\rho_s)
=\log\zeta(s)-s\frac{\zeta'(s)}{\zeta(s)}.}                 \tag{1}
$$

These are statements about a **chosen realization of the state as a
classical branch dynamics**. They are not Lyapunov exponents of the quantum
unitary group generated by `K_0`, and they do not show that a density matrix
uniquely determines a flow.

The ingredients have established precedents. The logarithmic Hamiltonian
and arithmetic isometries occur in the Bost--Connes framework; generalized
Lüroth maps realize prescribed branch probabilities as Bernoulli dynamics.
The contribution here is their explicit application to the manuscript,
including reversible evolution, an action/precision identity, independent
checks, and controls against overinterpretation. No claim of mathematical
priority is made.

  The operator conventions are also given in the research paper

- Barrionuevo, Burton, Dajani, and Kraaikamp,

## 2. What independent prime rotations cannot do

For finitely many modes with fixed frequencies,

$$
\theta_p(t)=\theta_p(0)+t\log p/\tau_0,
$$

the derivative of the phase flow with respect to its initial state is the
identity. Every Lyapunov exponent is zero. A smooth observable on this
compact phase torus has bounded derivative and cannot turn this into
sustained exponential separation of nearby phases.

The logarithms of distinct primes are rationally independent: an integer
relation between them would contradict unique factorization. This provides
quasiperiodicity, not a mechanism for positive Lyapunov exponents.

Adding finite measurement uncertainty does not change this fact. The
missing ingredient must be a different evolution law, an unbounded or
infinite-dimensional operation with its own analysis, or a singular
observation mechanism. The construction below supplies a different law.

## 3. An exact operator that turns prime action into a scale shift

Let `e_n(x)=exp(2 pi i n x)`, `n>=1`, be the positive Fourier basis on the
circle. Define

$$
K_0 e_n=(\log n)e_n,\qquad U_m e_n=e_{mn},\quad m\ge2.      \tag{2}
$$

On finite Fourier polynomials, hence on the appropriate operator domains,

$$
\begin{aligned}
U_m^*U_m&=1,\\
U_mU_m^*&=\text{projection onto modes divisible by }m,\\
U_mU_r&=U_{mr},\\
[K_0,U_m]&=(\log m)U_m,\\
e^{itK_0}U_m e^{-itK_0}&=m^{it}U_m.                       \tag{3}
\end{aligned}
$$

The last identity is a phase evolution **of the shift operator**. Applying
`U_m` repeatedly to a state or observable is a separate operation. These
two evolutions must not be identified.

For the real-space map

$$
T_m(x)=mx\pmod1,
$$

its Koopman pullback is exactly

$$
(U_mf)(x)=f(T_mx),\qquad U_me_n=e_{mn}.                    \tag{4}
$$

Thus the observable's frequency goes from `n` to `mn` to `m^2 n`, while
the classical tangent map expands by `m` per iteration. With respect to
Lebesgue measure,

$$
\lambda_+(T_m)=h_{\rm KS}(T_m)=\log m.                    \tag{5}
$$

For a prime `p`, Chapter 147's cycle action gives the exact numerical chain

$$
\boxed{\frac12\log\det{}'\Delta_{C_p}
=\log p
=\text{action increment under }U_p
=\lambda_+(T_p).}                                        \tag{6}
$$

The `p=2` cycle uses the two-parallel-edge convention of Chapter 147.
Equation (6) is a correspondence between constructed operators; the cycle
Laplacian has not dynamically generated `T_p`.

Composite controls `m=4,6` satisfy the same chain. Primes are irreducible
generators of the integer-dilation semigroup, since

$$
U_m=\prod_p U_p^{v_p(m)},
$$

but chaotic expansion does not require `m` to be prime.

### Normalization of the modular clock

On this Hilbert space the faithful normal Gibbs density exists for `s>1`:

$$
\rho_s=\frac{e^{-sK_0}}{\zeta(s)},\qquad
K_s=-\log\rho_s=sK_0+\log\zeta(s).
$$

With the manuscript's modular-flow sign convention,

$$
e^{itK_s}U_m e^{-itK_s}=m^{ist}U_m.                       \tag{7}
$$

The factor `s` matters. `exp(-K_0)` is not trace class, so `K_0` must not be
called the modular Hamiltonian of the nonexistent normalized `s=1` Gibbs
density. This does not rule out other representations or KMS states of a
larger algebra; it is a statement about this ordinary Hilbert-space trace.

## 4. Reversible chaos and the local boost matrix

The expanding circle map discards its branch label. An invertible extension
stores that label in a second coordinate. For `j=floor(mx)`, define

$$
B_m(x,y)=\left(mx-j,\frac{y+j}{m}\right).                  \tag{8}
$$

The inverse uses `j=floor(my')`:

$$
x=(x'+j)/m,\qquad y=my'-j.
$$

Apart from partition boundaries, the derivative is

$$
DB_m=\begin{pmatrix}m&0\\0&m^{-1}\end{pmatrix},\qquad
\det DB_m=1,\qquad \lambda_\pm=\pm\log m.                 \tag{9}
$$

Information revealed in the expanding coordinate is retained in the
contracting coordinate. Metric entropy measures information revealed per
step by a generating observation; it is not fine-grained information
destruction by the full invertible map.

The linear part of (9) is the same matrix as a Lorentz boost in null
coordinates, with rapidity `eta=log m`. It is also a canonical squeeze in
dimensionless phase-space coordinates. The folding and branch translations
make recurrent baker dynamics possible. Repeating a change of inertial
frame does not produce physical chaos: the phase-space squeeze and the
branch gluing are additional dynamics, and no identification with physical
spacetime has been proved.

There is a simple obstruction to removing all this gluing by using one
linear torus automorphism. A matrix in `SL(2,Z)` with eigenvalues `p,1/p`
would have integer trace `p+1/p`, impossible for integer `p>1`. This only
rules out that linear compactification, not all smooth chaotic systems
with the same numerical Lyapunov exponent.

## 5. The modular-to-chaotic realization theorem

Let positive weights `w_n` sum to one, with `max_n w_n<1` and finite Shannon
entropy. Put

$$
a_n=\sum_{j<n}w_j,\qquad I_n=[a_n,a_n+w_n).
$$

For `x in I_n`, define

$$
T_w(x)=\frac{x-a_n}{w_n},\qquad
B_w(x,y)=\left(\frac{x-a_n}{w_n},a_n+w_n y\right).          \tag{10}
$$

Here `T_w` is a generalized Lüroth map and `B_w` its baker extension.

**Proof of measure preservation.** For measurable `E subset [0,1)`, its
preimage in branch `n` has length `w_n |E|`; summing gives `|E|`. Each
vertical strip of the square maps bijectively to a horizontal strip, with
Jacobian determinant one. The inverse identifies `n` from the new `y`.

**Proof of Bernoulli coding.** The cylinder specified by a word
`(n_0,...,n_{r-1})` has length

$$
|C_{n_0\ldots n_{r-1}}|=\prod_{j=0}^{r-1}w_{n_j}.         \tag{11}
$$

These cylinders shrink to points because `max w_n<1`. Thus the branch
partition generates the interval dynamics, and under Lebesgue measure its
symbols are independent with probabilities `w_n`. The invertible extension
has the corresponding two-sided coding, up to null boundary exceptions.

**Proof of the action law.** For `rho=diag(w_n)`, its modular Hamiltonian
has eigenvalues `k_n=-log w_n`. Equation (10) gives

$$
\boxed{\log|T'_w(x)|=k_n\quad(x\in I_n).}                 \tag{12}
$$

Consequently, along an itinerary,

$$
\boxed{
\log|(T_w^r)'(x)|
=\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}k_{n_j}
=-\log|C_{n_0\ldots n_{r-1}}|.}                           \tag{13}
$$

As long as two points share that itinerary, their separation is multiplied
by the exponential of this accumulated action. Finite entropy permits the
ergodic average, yielding `lambda_+=S(rho)` almost everywhere. Bernoulli
coding independently gives `h_KS=S(rho)`. The baker map has opposite
exponents and zero sum.

This is an exact existence theorem. The choice of full-branch evolution in
(10) is essential, not a consequence of knowing the eigenvalues of `rho`.

### Branch amplitudes and probabilities

On `L^2([0,1))`, define

$$
(V_nf)(x)=w_n^{-1/2}{\bf1}_{I_n}(x)f(T_wx).
$$

They obey `V_n^*V_j=delta_nj 1` and `sum_n V_n V_n^*=1`. The ordinary
Koopman isometry and its transfer adjoint are

$$
U=\sum_n\sqrt{w_n}V_n,\qquad
(U^*f)(x)=\sum_n w_n f(a_n+w_nx).                          \tag{14}
$$

The sum defining `U` converges strongly. Thus amplitude `exp(-k_n/2)` and
probability `exp(-k_n)` are consistent with a genuine branch operator,
not merely with a chosen oscillatory kernel. They follow from the interval
geometry and its measure; quantum measurement physics has not been derived.

## 6. Zeta Gibbs weights give an explicit chaos rate

Use the state from Chapter 147, for real `s>1`:

$$
w_n=\frac{n^{-s}}{\zeta(s)},\qquad
k_n=s\log n+\log\zeta(s).                                \tag{15}
$$

The partition normalization is part of the local stretch. In particular,
the `n=1` branch has positive action `log zeta(s)`; its zero arithmetic
energy does not make its branch stretch vanish.

Since `sum n^-s log n` converges for `s>1`, the theorem applies and gives
(1). Unique factorization provides the independently equivalent prime sum

$$
\boxed{
S(\rho_s)=\sum_p\left[
-\log(1-p^{-s})+\frac{s\log p}{p^s-1}
\right].}                                               \tag{16}
$$

The prime occupation counts are independent geometric variables in this
Gibbs distribution. Equations (1) and (16) connect the thermodynamic
entropy to the dynamical entropy of the chosen realization.

As `s` approaches one from above, the entropy diverges; at or below one

## 7. An exact weighted dynamical zeta and pressure

The symbolic dynamics of (10) is a full shift. For real `q` with `s q>1`,
the branch moment is

$$
M_s(q)=\sum_n w_n^q=\frac{\zeta(sq)}{\zeta(s)^q}.          \tag{17}
$$

Sum the weights of all length-`r` periodic words. The result is `M_s(q)^r`.
Grouping words by primitive cycles proves, in its convergence domain,

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal Z_s(u,q)
&=\exp\left[\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{u^r}{r}M_s(q)^r\right]\\
&=\prod_{\gamma\ \mathrm{primitive}}\left(1-u^{|\gamma|}
\prod_{j\in\gamma}w_j^q\right)^{-1}\\
&=\boxed{\frac1{1-u\zeta(sq)/\zeta(s)^q}}.               \tag{18}
\end{aligned}
$$

For positive real parameters, `|u|M_s(q)<1` suffices. In (18), `j in gamma`
means each symbol occurrence, with multiplicity. The associated pressure is

$$
P_s(q)=\log\zeta(sq)-q\log\zeta(s),\qquad
P_s(1)=0,\qquad -P_s'(1)=S(\rho_s).                       \tag{19}
$$

The local arithmetic partition function now appears inside a genuine
weighted orbit-counting object. This is a full-shift construction, not a
Hilbert--Polya operator or a statement about Riemann zero locations.
In particular, at `s=2`, `q=1/2` is the divergence boundary of the moment
sum; it is not an RH critical-line result.

For finite alphabets, periodic-word counts and counts on a half-open
geometric interval can differ at endpoint orbits. The audit enumerates
**symbolic** primitive words. Separately, for the circle map `T_m`,
`#Fix(T_m^r)=m^r-1`, so its orbit-counting zeta is `(1-u)/(1-mu)` rather
than the full symbolic shift's `1/(1-mu)`. No endpoint correction is hidden.

Möbius inversion removes repeated words:

$$
a_r=\frac1r\sum_{d\mid r}\mu(d)N_{r/d}.
$$

It is the same Möbius function used in the manuscript, performing the
standard task of extracting primitive cycles.

## 8. What is predetermined and what finite observation loses

Equation (13) equates accumulated branch action with the precision needed
to specify an itinerary. A word cylinder of width `exp(-A_r)` is mapped
across the entire interval after `r` steps. Conditional on being uniformly
distributed in that cylinder, its image is uniform. This is an exact
conditional-ensemble statement, not an assertion that the exact point has
become random.

For typical trajectories in the zeta map, `A_r/r -> S(rho_s)`. For this
generating observation, the typical itinerary information cost is therefore
`r S(rho_s)/log 2` bits. A finite initial precision budget gives a typical
horizon, with trajectory-dependent fluctuations; it is not a universal
sharp bound for every exceptional initial condition.

There is also an exact Fourier version. Let `Pi_M` retain positive modes
`1,...,M`. Then

$$
\operatorname{rank}(\Pi_M U_m^r\Pi_M)=\lfloor M/m^r\rfloor. \tag{20}
$$

Once `m^r>M`, this compression is zero, although the full `U_m^r` remains
an isometry. Observable structure has moved beyond this bandwidth. Including
the constant Fourier mode leaves that mode intact; (20) concerns nonzero
modes only. Projection has discarded information, not the full dynamics.

An arithmetic endpoint shortcut exists for a precisely known rational input:

$$
T_m^r(a/b)=\frac{a m^r\bmod b}{b}.                        \tag{21}
$$

Modular exponentiation evaluates this using `O(log r)` modular
multiplications at fixed denominator bit length. The audit checks every
step through 1000 against direct iteration and verifies composition at
`r=10^18`. This special-map shortcut needs the exact rational input;
rational circle orbits are eventually periodic. It is not a shortcut for
generic nonlinear systems or uncertain initial data.

## 9. The static-state no-go and the missing order

The identity map on the same interval preserves Lebesgue measure and has
the same one-time branch probabilities `w_n`, while its entropy rate and
Lyapunov exponent are zero. The full-branch map has rate `S(w)`. Thus the
same spectral data admit different temporal laws.

For a stationary observed label process with these marginals,

$$
h_{\rm labels}\le H(w),
$$

with equality for independent successive labels. It becomes the full
dynamical metric entropy when this observation is generating. It is **not**
a universal bound on the entropy of hidden degrees of freedom or on every
physical system associated with the same density matrix.

There is a more specific obstruction to recovering a trajectory from
arithmetic action alone. The words `(2,3)` and `(3,2)` have the same product
and accumulated action, but different cylinders and different trajectories.
For the finite `n=1,...,4`, `s=2` audit, their intervals are

$$
C_{2,3}=[1440/1681,36576/42025),
$$

$$
C_{3,2}=[39204/42025,7956/8405).
$$

Both have width `576/42025`. The sum of logarithmic actions fixes the
stretching and cylinder size; it does not fix the cylinder's position or
symbol order. The branch isometries `V_n` retain ordered words, whereas a
commutative product of arithmetic dilation factors loses that order.

The revised missing object is an **ordered branch evolution derived from
the proposed physical coupling**, not simply a list of prime frequencies
or an equilibrium spectrum.

### An exact ordered extension: correlations subtract from the information rate

This missing object can be supplied explicitly in a controlled model.
Let a finite probability vector `w` be stationary for a strictly positive
transition matrix `P`: `sum_i w_i P_ij=w_j`. Split `I_i` into subintervals
`I_ij` of lengths `w_i P_ij`, with starts

$$
b_{ij}=a_i+w_i\sum_{k<j}P_{ik}.
$$

Map each `I_ij` onto `I_j`. Its derivative is

$$
T'_P|_{I_{ij}}=\frac{w_j}{w_iP_{ij}}>1.                  \tag{M1}
$$

The inequality follows from stationarity and strict positivity: each
joint contribution `w_i P_ij` is strictly smaller than its column sum
`w_j`. There are finitely many branches, so expansion is uniform. The
generating label process is precisely the stationary Markov chain `P`,
although each exact initial point follows a deterministic path.

There is again an area-preserving invertible extension. Write

$$
Q_{ji}=\frac{w_iP_{ij}}{w_j},\qquad
c_{ji}=\sum_{k<i}Q_{jk}.
$$

For `x in I_ij`, use

$$
B_P(x,y)=\left(a_j+\frac{x-b_{ij}}{Q_{ji}},
                     c_{ji}+Q_{ji}y\right).             \tag{M2}
$$

The new `x` identifies `j`; the new `y` identifies `i` in the reverse
transition partition `Q_j`. This gives the inverse and determinant one.

The local action now obeys a **conditional** law:

$$
\log T'_P|_{I_{ij}}
=-\log P_{ij}+\log w_j-\log w_i.                         \tag{M3}
$$

The marginal terms telescope along a path, and cancel under the stationary
average. Thus

$$
\boxed{
\lambda_+=h_{\rm KS}
=-\sum_{ij}w_iP_{ij}\log P_{ij}
=S(w)-I(X_t;X_{t+1}).}                                  \tag{M4}
$$

Equation (M4) is exact for this stationary Markov realization. It is also
the expected one-step log-loss improvement from conditioning on the
current label instead of forecasting with the marginal weights alone.
For a general stationary process with memory, replace the single current
label by the entire past when computing the entropy rate; pairwise mutual
information alone need not suffice.

This gives a precise use for previously unseen organization: temporal
correlations reduce the fresh information that must be resolved at each
step. It does not eliminate the residual positive exponent or reconstruct
an exact trajectory from finite data.

For a fixed marginal state, an explicit family is

$$
P_{ij}(r)=(1-r)\delta_{ij}+r w_j,\qquad 0<r\le1.          \tag{M5}
$$

At `r=1` it reproduces independent branch labels and entropy rate `S(w)`.
As `r` tends to zero the rate tends to zero; at zero the limiting
deterministic map is the identity. Hence the same state admits a continuous
range of temporal information rates. The term "refresh" is a parameter of
the transition law, not an injection of random noise into the map.

For Chapter 149's `p=3` state, take `w=(3/4,1/4)` and `r=1/4`:

$$
P=\begin{pmatrix}15/16&1/16\\3/16&13/16\end{pmatrix}.
$$

The audit finds, in nats per iteration,

$$
S(w)=0.562335144619,\qquad
\lambda_+=h_{\rm KS}=0.295988135324,\qquad
I=0.266347009295.
$$

Its subleading eigenvalue is `1-r=3/4`, so a forecast from just the initial
label is

$$
(P^k)_{ij}=w_j+(3/4)^k(\delta_{ij}-w_j).                  \tag{M6}
$$

Starting in the rarer branch, its probability after 1, 2, 5, and 10 steps
is respectively `0.8125`, `0.671875`, `0.427978515625`, and
`0.2922351360321045`. A state-only forecast always returns `0.25`.
The spectral forecast uses no intermediate observations. The executable
validates it against an independent exact sum over all future symbolic
paths. This is a prediction inside the constructed model, not a fitted
result on Lorenz or three-body data.

### The ordered cycle determinant retains that predictive memory

For the entrywise power matrix `(M_q)_ij=P_ij^q`, primitive ordered cycles
satisfy

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal Z_P(u,q)
=\exp\sum_{k\ge1}\frac{u^k}{k}\operatorname{tr}(M_q^k)
=\det(1-uM_q)^{-1}.}                                    \tag{M7}
$$

This is a finite-matrix identity, valid as a convergent expansion when
`|u| rho(M_q)<1`, or as a formal power series. At `q=1`, the two-state
example has

$$
\mathcal Z_P(u,1)=\frac1{(1-u)(1-3u/4)}.
$$

The second factor retains the transition law's memory timescale. With independent
rows `P_ij=w_j`, the matrix has rank one and the determinant reduces to
`[1-u sum_j w_j^q]^-1`, recovering (18) for zeta weights. For the positive
finite matrix, the pressure `log rho(M_q)` has derivative `-h_KS` at
`q=1`. Thus an ordered transfer determinant carries information that the
marginal arithmetic partition function omits.

## 10. A correction specific to Chapter 149's prime cell

The two-state density is

$$
\sigma_p=\operatorname{diag}\left(\frac p{p+1},\frac1{p+1}\right).
$$

Its modular gap is `log p`, but the corresponding **two-branch** baker
realization has

$$
\boxed{\lambda_+(p)=S(\sigma_p)
=\log(p+1)-\frac p{p+1}\log p.}                           \tag{22}
$$

This decreases with `p`, since its derivative is
`-log p/(p+1)^2`. It must not be confused with the equal-width **p-branch**
map `T_p`, whose rate is `log p`.

| `p` | Modular gap | Two-branch entropy/positive exponent |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2 | 0.693147 | 0.636514 |
| 3 | 1.098612 | 0.562335 |
| 5 | 1.609438 | 0.450561 |
| 11 | 2.397895 | 0.286836 |
| 157 | 5.056246 | 0.038351 |

A larger gap biases the state toward one branch. Even in the maximal
Bernoulli realization of these marginals, less information is revealed per
iteration. This is a conditional dynamical interpretation of the
wave/structure trade; it is not a general law identifying action with chaos.

## 11. Audit results and corrections to the older experiments

The executable passes these independent checks:

- Fourier pullback versus mode dilation, cycle pseudo-determinant versus
  logarithmic action, and modular covariance including its factor of `s`.
- Direct necklace enumeration versus Möbius inversion, for alphabets of
  size two and three through word length six.
- Exact rational multiplication of primitive-cycle Euler factors versus
  independently summed word weights, through degree six at moments one
  and two.
- One hundred exact rational forward steps followed by one hundred inverse
  steps, recovering both initial coordinates exactly.
- A `10^-30` initial perturbation, with the exact action/separation law
  verified on all 84 shared-itinerary steps. The first branch difference
  occurs at step 85; separation at step 100 is approximately `0.449349`.
- All 256 four-symbol cylinders of the four-branch test have their claimed
  exact rational widths.
- Zeta and entropy evaluation by Euler--Maclaurin at two cutoffs, known
  even-zeta values, and independent integral bounds on positive series.
  The bounds are evaluated in floating point, not certified outward-rounded
  interval arithmetic.
- A separate prime entropy sum through prime cutoff 100000; at `s=2` its
  missing positive tail is approximately `2.07682e-5` nats.
- The exact purity `zeta(4)/zeta(2)^2=2/5`, and a finite-difference check of
  the pressure derivative against the entropy.
- Composite dilation factors, zero-chaos dynamics with the same marginals,
  reversed symbol order, exact modular endpoint jumps, and loss of a known
  base-three prefix in a finite uncertainty ensemble.
- A second 100-step exact reversible trajectory for the ordered Markov
  baker map, with the conditional action law checked by exact telescoping.
  Its independent primitive-cycle product agrees with the transition
  determinant through degree six at moments one and two; spectral forecasts
  agree exactly with enumeration through ten future steps.

The infinite identities are justified by the derivations above. The audit
checks finite instances and numerical consequences; it does not numerically
prove a statement at infinity.

The originating repository's older `constructive_interference_3bp.py`
constructs each field from the already simulated true positions at that
time. Its tracking result is a reconstruction test. It is not evidence of
autonomous prime-mode forecasting. Chapters 113--115's stronger claims
about resolving chaos are not established by that experiment.

Chaos is an intrinsic property of an evolution law; measurement precision
controls predictability. Neither delayed signaling nor a Planck-scale
information limit is needed for the mathematical chaos proved here. A
Newtonian force evaluated at delayed positions is also not, by itself, a
derivation of relativistic gravity.

## 12. The next discriminating experiment

The binary forecast portion proposed here has subsequently been carried
out in [the external benchmark](BENCHMARK_RESULTS.md). The nonlinear
identity is proved in [NONLINEAR_ACTION.md](NONLINEAR_ACTION.md). Deriving
a physically selected arithmetic transition operator remains open.

The construction establishes existence of arithmetic chaotic models. To
show utility for an independently specified system, the next test must
derive or estimate an ordered evolution from that system's available
initial data, then evaluate it on withheld future data.

For example, start with a Poincaré return map of Lorenz dynamics and seek a
generating or approximately generating partition. Infer its branch weights,
transition constraints, return times, and local stretch from training data.
